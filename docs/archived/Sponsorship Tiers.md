## 2. Sponsorship Tier Matrix (Historical Reference)

### Tier 1: Archimedes Tier (Title Sponsor)
- **Contribution Level:** ₹1,20,000
- **Deliverables:**
  - Complete co-branding: *"[Brand] and RDEC present SYNTAXIS 2026"*.
  - Prime logo position on stage backdrops, print banners, participant lanyards, and certificates.
  - Verbal stage announcement at every major assembly.
  - Naming rights to one flagship contest or workshop.
  - Dedicated campus stall space for all three days.
  - Full post-event analytical report, digital reach statistics, and photo archive.

### Tier 2: Euclid Tier (Co-Sponsor)
- **Contribution Level:** ₹70,000
- **Deliverables:**
  - Association co-branding: *"SYNTAXIS 2026 in association with [Brand]"*.
  - Secondary logo position on all primary banners and backdrop collaterals.
  - Stage recognition during The Kindling (Inauguration) and Apotheosis (Valediction).
  - Dedicated stall space on campus.
  - Minimum three dedicated social media activations.

### Tier 3: Associate & Track Sponsors
- **Contribution Level:** ₹25,000 – ₹40,000 (or equivalent verified in-kind tool access/swag)
- **Deliverables:**
  - Logo presence on the official website and event banners.
  - Dedicated product or tool showcase access for participants.
  - Official certificate of institutional partnership.
