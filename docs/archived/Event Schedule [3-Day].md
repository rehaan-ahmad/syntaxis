# SYNTAXIS 2026: Master Event Schedule

> **Archive Notice:** This document is an archived record of the finalized three-day master schedule for SYNTAXIS 2026. All times and venues match the festival web application.

- **Festival:** SYNTAXIS 2026
- **Dates:** October 29 – 31, 2026
- **Location:** R.D. Engineering College (RDEC), Duhai, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201206
- **Standard Operating Hours:** 08:30 AM – 06:00 PM IST

---

## Day 1: Thursday, October 29, 2026
### Foundation, Inauguration, Techne & Code Trilogy Round 1

Day 1 establishes the operational and technical foundation of the festival. Activities include attendee verification, the official inaugural ceremony, hands-on cloud instruction, and algorithmic problem-solving.

| Time Window | Event Title | Discipline / Track | Venue Location | Operational Notes |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 08:30 AM – 10:00 AM | Participant Check-In & Badge Collection | General Operations | Main Campus Gate | Registration desk verification, official lanyard distribution, and venue orientation. |
| 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM | The Kindling (Inauguration Ceremony) | Festival Ceremony | Main Stage, College Grounds | Traditional lamp lighting, welcome address by institute leadership, and the official opening of Syntaxis 2026. |
| 11:30 AM – 01:30 PM | Techne (Google Cloud Skill Badges) | Cloud & DevOps Workshop | DSA Lab, Basement A-Block | Hands-on cloud architecture workshop focused on earning Google Cloud Skill Badges, Docker containers, and live deployment. |
| 01:30 PM – 04:30 PM | Code Trilogy — Round 1: HEUREKA | Competitive Programming | Central Computer Center (Lab 1 & 2) | Data structures and algorithmic problem solving contest hosted on the GeeksforGeeks platform. |
| 04:30 PM – 05:30 PM | Day 1 Wrap-up & Day 2 Briefing | Daily Assembly | Main Stage | Day 1 score compilation, Day 2 check-in reminders, and overnight hackathon prep instructions. |

## Day 2: Friday, October 30, 2026
### The Crucible: Archithon 24h, Contests, Rhesis & Pantheon Arena

Day 2 focuses on technical endurance, keynote discourse, and esports competition. The flagship overnight hackathon begins.

| Time Window | Event Title | Discipline / Track | Venue Location | Operational Notes |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 08:30 AM – 09:30 AM | Archithon Check-In & Lab Allocation | Flagship Hackathon | IT Block, 2nd Floor Labs | Hardware safety checks, mentor desk assignment, and initial team workspace setup. |
| 09:30 AM – 10:00 AM | Archithon Opening Briefing & Problem Statements | Flagship Hackathon | Seminar Hall 1 | Formal release of track problem statements and judging rubrics. |
| 10:00 AM (Starts) | Archithon 24h Hackathon Begins | Flagship Hackathon | Designated Software Labs (2nd Floor) | 24-hour continuous build clock starts. Round 1 jury inspection starts at 04:00 PM. |
| 10:30 AM – 12:30 PM | Code Trilogy — Round 2: AGON | Competitive Programming | Central Computer Center (Lab 1) | High-speed algorithmic sprint hosted on the HackerRank platform. |
| 12:30 PM – 01:30 PM | Lunch Break | General Operations | College Cafeteria | Scheduled meal break for attendees, mentors, and registered participants. |
| 01:30 PM – 03:30 PM | Code Trilogy — Round 3: KATHARSIS | Debugging Duel | Central Computer Center (Lab 2) | Reverse-debugging challenge where pairs isolate and repair planted defects in complex codebases. |
| 02:00 PM – 04:00 PM | Rhesis (Keynote Talks & Panel Discussion) | Discourse & Leadership | Main Auditorium | Industry leaders deliver keynotes and interactive panels covering modern engineering and career growth. |
| 02:30 PM – 05:30 PM | Pantheon Games (Free Fire Esports Arena) | Mobile Esports | Seminar Hall 2 | Squad battle royale tournament arena on dedicated low-latency gaming networks. |
| 09:00 PM – 10:30 PM | Archithon Midnight Review (Milestone 2) | Flagship Hackathon | Hackathon Labs | Architecture and progress verification by industry mentors. |

## Day 3: Saturday, October 31, 2026
### Archithon Demos, Tribunal Jury Defense & Apotheosis Valedictory

Day 3 concludes the festival arc with hackathon project defense, grand jury evaluation, and the awards distribution ceremony.

| Time Window | Event Title | Discipline / Track | Venue Location | Operational Notes |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 08:00 AM – 09:30 AM | Breakfast & Final Sprint | Hackathon Support | College Cafeteria / Labs | Final morning sprint for hackathon teams before the development freeze. |
| 10:00 AM | Archithon Code Freeze & Submission | Flagship Hackathon | Software Labs | The development clock ends. Git repositories lock and project submissions freeze. |
| 10:30 AM – 01:00 PM | Archithon Preliminary Demos & Shortlisting | Technical Evaluation | IT Block Labs | All teams demonstrate working software to evaluation panels. Top finalists advance to the stage. |
| 01:00 PM – 02:00 PM | Lunch Break | General Operations | College Cafeteria | Scheduled meal break before the grand afternoon assembly. |
| 02:00 PM – 03:30 PM | The Tribunal (Grand Jury Defense) | Stage Presentations | Main Auditorium | Finalist teams present live software architecture demonstrations and answer jury cross-examination. |
| 03:30 PM – 05:00 PM | Apotheosis (Valedictory & Awards Ceremony) | Festival Ceremony | Main Auditorium | Institute address, felicitation of title and co-sponsors, and prize distribution for all tracks. |
| 05:00 PM – 05:30 PM | Epilogos (Official Closing Remarks) | Concluding Assembly | Main Auditorium | Concluding address by Fest Directors Rehaan Ahmad & Diya Sharma. Official closure of Syntaxis 2026. |
