## **Overview: What is SYNTAXIS?**

*Syntaxis is an intensive 3-day inter-college technical festival organized by the Nexora Tech Club at RDEC, Ghaziabad.*

* **Fest Identity & Scale**
  * **Theme:** Automate. Innovate. Elevate. (Derived from Greek root *syntassein* — "to arrange in order").
  * **Footfall & Reach:** 500–700+ participants across 25+ NCR colleges and 5+ partner schools.
  * **Events:** 8+ events spanning competitive programming, hackathons, TEDx-style talks, and the Genesis Track.
  * **Leadership:** Driven by industry-leading experts and 2026 Google Student Ambassadors.
* **Platform & Digital Reach**
  * **Instagram (GfG RDEC Chapter + RDEC Official Handles):** Reels, countdowns, event reveals, sponsor features, and winner posts.
  * **LinkedIn & WhatsApp:** Direct outreach to 25+ college communities for sponsor features, speaker announcements, post-event coverage, and registration links.

## **Sponsorship Terms & Agreement**

*Standard operating rules and policies governing all sponsorship commitments.*

* **Payment Method**
  * Bank transfer or UPI only. Account details provided securely once tier is locked and agreement is signed.
* **In-Kind Contributions**
  * Monetary value must equal or exceed tier minimum (assessed at MRP). Perishables not accepted. Digital goods must have minimum 3-month expiry post-fest.
* **Formal Agreement**
  * Sponsorship agreement sent on official RDEC letterhead. Production of marketing materials begins after agreement is signed and payment received.
* **Non-Refundable Policy**
  * All contributions are non-refundable. If fest is cancelled due to force majeure, sponsor receives full digital visibility package post-date and priority placement in next edition.
